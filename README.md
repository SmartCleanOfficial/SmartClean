"A few months ago, I was practicing data cleaning to prepare for a freelancing job I wanted to apply for. But every single day, I found myself completely frustrated and stressed out.Dealing with common data errors in large datasets was a nightmare. Writing complex formulas over and over again was exhausting, and trying to clean errors one by one was incredibly difficult.That's when I decided: why not just build my own data cleaning tool? I wanted to create something that would save people from going through the exact same stressful experience I had. So, I built the SmartClean add-on in Google Sheets to help people who have the same experience as me with data cleaning. It fixes large data instantly without formulas, and it’s completely free to try!"
